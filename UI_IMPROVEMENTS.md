# UI Improvements

## Done

- Torch-lit dungeon arena: procedurally painted flagstone floor with cracks and grime, brick back wall with a ledge, baked warm lighting, and a vignette.
- Live fire: flickering wall sconces and floor braziers that cast additive light on actors and release embers.
- Animated characters replace rectangles: an armored knight (helmet, plume, cape, tabard, sword) and per-level monsters (goblin, ghoul, orc, demon) with walk cycles, idle breathing, and soft contact shadows.
- Combat feedback: sword slash trails, enemy lunges, hit flashes, sparks, blood droplets that leave floor stains, popping damage numbers, screen shake, red damage vignette, and enemies that topple and fade on death.
- Shield shows as a raised shield plus a shimmering ward.
- HUD rebuilt as dark leather-and-gold panels: player portrait and health bar with a trailing damage segment, level banner with foes remaining, ability slots with radial cooldown, shield timer ring, charge badge, and keycap hints.
- Cinematic menu, game-over, and victory screens with metallic title lettering over the live arena.

## Next

- Swap procedural art for hand-drawn sprite sheets if/when assets exist.
- Sound: sword swings, impacts, torch crackle.
- Pause/settings overlay for volume, controls, and debug display.
